# Telco Customer Churn Prediction

This project explores customer churn in a telecommunications business. It takes customer account and service information, prepares the data for analysis, and uses machine-learning techniques to estimate which customers may be at risk of leaving.

The goal is not only to build a predictive model, but also to understand the factors behind churn. The results can help a telecom company identify customers who may need better support, a more suitable plan, or a timely retention offer.

## What this project covers

- Cleaning and preparing the customer data
- Exploring patterns in customer behavior and churn
- Transforming categorical and numerical features for modeling
- Training and evaluating a churn-prediction model
- Reviewing the results and considering how they could support business decisions
